About

I build Full Stack Webs, backend systems, APIs and Telegram bots, mostly with Node.js, Express and MongoDB.

Most of what's here started as something I needed myself: a way to verify payments without paying for a gateway contract, a bot that could message thousands of users without tripping Telegram's rate limits, an API that could check a crypto transaction without me opening a block explorer by hand each time.

The project people ask about most is CWMPay, a payment verification platform I ran for about two weeks. It grew to 50 to 60 active merchants and processed more than ₹40,000 in real transactions, entirely by word of mouth. I paused it once I found a regulatory gap in how the money moved, and didn't think it was right to keep scaling before fixing that.

I'm currently open for freelance and contract work. Here's what I can help with, or you can just get in touch.
